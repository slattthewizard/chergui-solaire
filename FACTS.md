## 0. FRAMING RULES THAT BIND EVERY NUMBER

1. A price is what ONE named seller or publisher lists, never "le prix au Maroc". Always name the source and the
   month: "Briconet affichait un kit raccordé de 3 kW à 16 000 DH en octobre 2026". Never present a figure as this
   site's devis.
2. Say HT or TTC when the source says it; when it does not, write "TVA non précisée par le vendeur".
3. Never blend sources into one average. Published installed ranges disagree by a factor of up to four: show the
   spread and explain why (kit only vs fourni-posé, battery or not, panel brand, roof type).
4. A current written devis listing exact panel, inverter and battery models is the real number. Every price section
   says so.
5. Amounts in DH (e.g. 35 000 DH). kWc for panel power, kW for inverters, kWh for energy and battery capacity.

## 5. THE LAW: SELF-PRODUCTION (Law 82-21, text read in Bulletin officiel n° 7400)

- Law 82-21 on the self-production of electricity, promulgated by Dahir n° 1-23-21 of 10 February 2023 (French
  text published in BO n° 7400). Source PDF: anre.ma.
- Art. 4: an installation below a power threshold set by decree, connected to the low-voltage network, is DECLARED to
  the electricity distribution operator concerned BEFORE work starts.
- Art. 5: from that threshold up to 5 MW, a connection agreement (accord de raccordement) is needed before work.
- Art. 10: the grid operator may not stop supplying a customer because the customer has a self-production installation.
- Art. 11: several installations on one site are added together to compute the power.
- Art. 12: the self-producer may sell "une proportion n'excédant pas 20 % de la production annuelle" as surplus to the
  grid operator, at a tariff set by ANRE.
- Art. 13: grid-connected self-producers pay a contribution for system and distribution services, set by ANRE.
- The transmission operator may curtail (écrêter) self-produced power within a regulatory threshold without compensation.
- Art. 28: building, operating or increasing the power of an installation without the required declaration is fined
  2 000 to 5 000 DH.
- Threshold and date (law-firm and press reports of decree n° 2-25-100 of 5 March 2026, NOT the decree text): under
  11 kW on low voltage = declaration, with a receipt within one month (Bird & Bird); entry into force in June 2026
  (Bird & Bird says 5 June, other sources 9 June: write "depuis juin 2026"). Always attribute the 11 kW figure:
  "selon le décret d'application, tel que rapporté par le cabinet Bird & Bird".

## 6. SURPLUS: WHAT A HOME CAN AND CANNOT EXPECT (ANRE news item, 19 Feb 2026)

- ANRE set the surplus tariff for 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027 at 21 centimes/kWh in peak hours and
  18 centimes/kWh off-peak, for high, very high and MEDIUM voltage networks only.
- ANRE: the low-voltage tariff "will be set by ANRE after the appropriate regulatory and technical framework has been
  put in place." So a house on low voltage has NO published buy-back price today.
- Write plainly: a home installation is sized to be used on site; exported surplus is capped at 20% of annual
  production by law and has no published low-voltage price yet. This is why batteries and daytime use matter.
- NEVER write net-metering, "revendre à l'ONEE", 0.85 MAD/kWh or a 5 kWc limit (claims found on a credit site,
  contradicted by the law).

## 9. CHOOSING AN INSTALLER

- AMEE's TaqaPro label is "le label Qualité pour les installateurs photovoltaïques au Maroc" (installateur.taqapro.amee.ma).
  TaqaPro PV covers installations up to 20 kW (self-consumption and off-grid): the firm must be over one year old,
  have at least one employee with PV knowledge and 2 years' experience, pass the TaqaPro PV training and final test,
  and sign an ethics charter; 2 000 DH TTC per trained representative. TaqaPro PV+ covers 20 kW to 2 MW. There is also
  a TaqaPro solar pumping strand. AMEE also runs ThermoPro for solar water heater installers.
- The labels are VOLUNTARY. No legal requirement for an installer licence or label was found in Law 82-21. Never
  write "obligatoire", "agréé" or "certifié" about installers; write "demandez si l'entreprise est labellisée TaqaPro
  et vérifiez l'annuaire sur taqapro.amee.ma".
- Ask every devis for: exact panel, inverter and battery models; HT/TTC; what installation includes (structure,
  cabling, protections, commissioning, the declaration to the distributor); payment schedule; warranties per component.

## 10. FINANCING AND SUBSIDIES

- No home-solar loan product was confirmed on any Moroccan bank's own site. Do NOT name bank products or rates
  (a credit site lists some; none were found at the banks).
- No national subsidy for household PV was found in an official source. Never claim one.
- Société Générale Maroc's "Pack Solaire" (2024) targets businesses, not households; no rate published.

## 11. DO NOT ASSERT

- Any household electricity tariff or bill saving in DH; any payback period or ROI.
- Net-metering, selling surplus from a house today, 0.85 MAD/kWh, a 5 kWc cap.
- "6 000-10 000 DH/kWc" (source not found). Any single national average price.
- Bank product names or rates; any state subsidy (PV, water heaters, pumping).
- That installers must be certified, licensed or "agréés"; that this site or any installer is labelled.
- The decree's exact date beyond "juin 2026"; any decree text quotation.
- Water-law rules for wells and boreholes (none sourced): say the basin agency (agence de bassin hydraulique) and the
  installer will advise on authorisations.
- Storage capacity of the Briconet off-grid 5 kW kit.
